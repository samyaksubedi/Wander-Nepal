Three beautiful places in Nepal

Each card has a short description, a general location, and a link that opens the place in Google Maps.

⌖ Pokhara, Kaski

Phewa Lake

Pokhara’s best-known lake is a calm place for boating, sunset views, and looking toward the Annapurna range. You can also take a boat to the small Tal Barahi Temple on its island.

Open in Google Maps ↗ Photo: Rajivkilanashrestha / CC BY-SA 4.0

⌖ Sauraha, Chitwan

Chitwan National Park

A UNESCO natural heritage site in Nepal’s lowlands, Chitwan is known for jungle safaris, birdwatching, river activities, and the chance to spot one-horned rhinos.

Open in Google Maps ↗ Photo: Anupama Rajya Shahi / CC0

⌖ Boudha, Kathmandu

Boudhanath Stupa

One of Kathmandu’s most recognisable landmarks, Boudhanath is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and an important centre of Himalayan Buddhism, surrounded by prayer wheels and monasteries.

Open in Google Maps ↗ Photo: Dudva / CC0
